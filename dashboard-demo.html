Parent app

Reassurance through relevant, policy-based updates.

Parents receive only the alerts the institution has configured and the guardian is entitled to receive: attendance updates, approved arrival/exit information, and response-status updates when escalation rules require it.

Notification design ruleUse clear context, a timestamp, next action and an acknowledgement route—not alarming or vague alerts.

Safety response design

SOS that is deliberate, clear and accountable.

A robust emergency control must never pretend to replace emergency services or the college’s response plan. This prototype illustrates a product flow only.

HoldStudent holds the SOS button for 3 seconds to reduce accidental activation.
ConfirmA clear screen gives a short cancel window and shows what will be shared.
RouteThe configured authorised responders receive the case; guardian alerts follow policy.
RecordStatus changes and closure are restricted to authorised responders.
